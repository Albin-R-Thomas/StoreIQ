# Store IQ Demo Video Script

**Starting point:** Already signed in as the store manager. The website labels this role Store administrator and provides Store management.

**Target runtime:** 4 minutes 40 seconds, including transitions. Keep the final export under 5 minutes.

## Setup to keep prepared

- Keep your current manager session open on **Overview**. Start the recording here; no login demonstration is needed.
- Open an associate session in a separate browser profile or private window. Use a working associate account. Demo defaults, when enabled, are mobile `9000000001` and password `Associate@123`. Keep password entry out of the recording.
- Keep **Store management** ready in the manager session. Identify one TV record with stock and pricing, an offer with its validity dates, and an approved knowledge record to show. You do not need to change data during the recording.
- In the associate session, rehearse the customer journey with synthetic data. Check AI responses and source display before recording. If using voice, test microphone permission and translation first; a live voice segment is optional.
- Prepare a comparison with two or three actual in-stock TVs that fit your test customer's requirements. Use the displayed model names, prices, offers, and totals; do not memorise values that may change.
- Prepare a separate test visit with home confirmation, demo preference, and site checks completed so its quote is ready. Also prepare an after-sale test visit with a PDF summary and follow-through tasks. Label cuts to these visits **Prepared demo stage**.
- Keep a saved expert request, visit history, and coaching review ready to show. Use simulated evidence for demo and task completion examples; do not imply a physical demonstration happened on screen.
- A test sale changes synthetic stock. Ensure sufficient demo stock for the final take, or use a fresh isolated demo dataset.
- Close unrelated tabs, hide secrets and real customer information, disable notifications, and set a readable browser zoom. Test the screen recorder and microphone.
- Trim idle loading time while preserving the actual input and response. Never insert a fabricated AI answer. Rehearse once with a timer before the final recording.

## Text to keep ready

**Customer requirements**

> My budget is ₹60,000. We sit nine feet from the TV in a bright living room. We mostly watch cricket and movies, use streaming apps, and want wall mounting.

**Product question**

> Does 4K automatically mean better picture quality?

**Simulated demonstration evidence**

> For this simulated demo, the customer preferred [actual selected model] because cricket motion looked clearer and the picture was easier to see in the bright room.

Replace the model placeholder before use. Answer any missing questions and confirm the captured home details before requesting recommendations. Use the contextual buttons where available.

## Recording script

### 0:00 to 0:25  Introduce the manager workspace

**Show:** Your current manager session on Overview, then open Store management.

**Say:**

“This is Store IQ, an AI-assisted workspace for TV store teams. I am signed in as the store manager. From here, I can maintain the information associates use and oversee saved customer activity. Let's see how that connects to the customer journey.”

### 0:25 to 1:00  Show store management

**Show:** One catalogue record, stock and pricing, a dated offer, and approved knowledge. Browse without changing values.

**Say:**

“Store management brings together TV specifications, stock, prices, offers, charges, finance terms, and approved knowledge. Associates use this information when explaining products and preparing quotes.

“Commercial details such as stock and prices are read live. Stable product and knowledge changes can be reflected in the search index through Rebuild RAG index.”

**Recording cue:** Show the rebuild control without triggering a rebuild during the video.

### 1:00 to 1:45  Switch to the associate journey

**Show:** Switch to the prepared associate browser window. Briefly show the shift brief, then open a new test customer and paste the requirements. Confirm the captured home details.

**Say:**

“Now I am switching to an associate session. The shift brief shows current stock and scheduled offers before the associate meets a customer.

“For this example, the customer has a sixty-thousand-rupee budget, sits nine feet away, and watches cricket and movies in a bright room. Store IQ captures the home requirements and asks for missing details one question at a time. Conversations support English, Hindi, and Marathi.”

### 1:45 to 2:25  Recommend and explain

**Show:** Recommendations, Compare TVs, and the answer to the prepared 4K question with its sources. If cutting to a prepared visit, display Prepared demo stage.

**Say:**

“After the home is confirmed, Store IQ ranks suitable in-stock options. The associate can compare televisions and explain differences for this customer's needs.

“Here, we ask whether 4K automatically means better picture quality. The answer uses available store knowledge and shows supporting sources. An unresolved question can be sent to an expert.”

### 2:25 to 3:10  Demonstration and complete cost

**Show:** Prepared quote visit. Display the recorded model preference and reason, site checks, required charges, optional extras, and available finance details.

**Say:**

“After a physical demonstration, the associate records the customer's preferred model and why they chose it. This example uses a simulated demonstration record.

“The site check and itemised quote make the full cost visible. Required charges and optional extras are separate, with applicable offers and finance calculations. Unknown required details hold the final total. Recording a sale requires explicit confirmation of the displayed amount.”

### 3:10 to 3:50  Summary and follow through

**Show:** Prepared after-sale visit, summary preview, PDF download, consent controls, and Follow-through tasks.

**Say:**

“The customer summary can be previewed and downloaded as a PDF. Sharing requires consent and a preview; external message delivery is simulated in this submission.

“Delivery, installation, and the working check remain separate tasks. Associates record completion evidence, and an installation problem can reopen follow-through.”

### 3:50 to 4:25  Return to the manager

**Show:** Switch back to the manager session. Show a saved expert request and attributed answer, then Visit history or coaching feedback.

**Say:**

“Back in the manager session, expert requests help resolve questions with answers attached to the customer's visit. Saved history preserves conversations and decisions.

“Coaching reviews use recorded evidence to identify missing steps, while the Learning studio supports repeat practice.”

### 4:25 to 4:40  Close

**Show:** Overview or a clear customer summary. Add the website URL as a closing caption if available.

**Say:**

“Store IQ connects store management with customer understanding, product guidance, transparent pricing, and after-sales follow-through. This demonstration uses synthetic data and test sales.”
