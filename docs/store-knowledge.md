## Display technology · what to show

Reference: `display-guide:0`

4K is 3840 by 2160 pixels and does not alone determine contrast or reflection handling. OLED has individually emitting pixels for dark-scene contrast; discuss static-image usage and demonstrate bright-room performance. Mini LED is an LCD with smaller backlight zones; check blooming with subtitles. QLED is an LCD using a quantum-dot layer for colour. Higher native refresh rates help compare fast motion. VRR synchronises compatible gaming sources. ALLM switches supported inputs into low-latency mode. Compare side seats when the family sits off-centre. Nits describe brightness; the demo catalogue is synthetic.
